About ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) is a technical institution located at Sangivalasa, Bheemunipatnam, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.

ANITS was established in the academic year 2001-02 and provides technical education in several engineering disciplines.

The institute is permanently affiliated with Andhra University and has programs in areas such as Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.
