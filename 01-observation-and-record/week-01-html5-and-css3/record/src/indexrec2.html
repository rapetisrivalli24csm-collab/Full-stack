01 / ABOUT

A place to learn,
build and grow.

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences is an engineering institution located at Sangivalasa, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.

The institute provides an academic environment for engineering education, practical learning, projects and professional development.

Students can develop technical knowledge while participating in laboratory activities, academic projects and other learning experiences.
