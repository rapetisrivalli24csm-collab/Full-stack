About ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is a technical institution situated in Sangivalasa, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.

The institution focuses on providing students with quality education, technical knowledge, practical skills, and opportunities for professional development.

ANITS encourages students to develop technical skills, creative thinking, and professional abilities.

Vision

To provide quality technical education and develop competent professionals who can contribute to society and the technological development of the nation.

Academic Departments

ANITS offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various engineering and technology disciplines.

Major Engineering Departments

  • Computer Science and Engineering
  • Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Information Technology

Academic Programs

Students can pursue different programs in engineering and technology according to their interests.

Student Academic Journey

  1. Choose an appropriate engineering discipline.
  2. Learn fundamental engineering concepts.
  3. Develop practical and technical skills.
  4. Participate in laboratory and project work.
  5. Complete academic and project requirements.
